Our approach

Every business works its own way. Off-the-shelf software asks you to change that. We do the opposite: we start with how you work today, then design and build the tools that take you where you want to go.

Some clients only need a website or an app. Others want a partner for the long run. Either way, it starts with understanding your business.

From origin to north.

The same five steps for a small website and a large system. Only the size changes.

  1. 01Origin

    Understand how you work

    We learn how your business runs today: the people, the processes, the tools, and the problems worth solving.

  2. 02Direction

    Agree on the plan

    Together we decide what to build first, what it should achieve and what it will take. You see a clear plan before any code is written.

  3. 03Build

    Design and build in short rounds

    You see real, working progress often and can steer as we go. No black box, no surprises at the end.

  4. 04Launch

    Go live calmly

    We launch, move your data across and help your team get comfortable with the new tools.

  5. 05North

    Keep improving

    We support, maintain and improve what we've built, and take on more of the work around it when you want us to.

Questions, answered.

Can't find yours? Ask us directly at info@originnorth.dk.

What does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. A focused website costs far less than a custom system connected to several other tools. After a first conversation we give you a clear estimate and suggest where to start, so you can decide with real numbers in front of you.

How long does it take?

A website can often go live within a few weeks. Larger systems are usually built and released in stages, so you start getting value early instead of waiting for everything at once.

We already use other software. Can you work with it?

Often the best solution connects the tools you already rely on, such as accounting, CRM, webshop or calendar systems, instead of replacing them. We look at what you have before suggesting anything new.

We're not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You know your business, and that's what we need from you. We explain choices in plain language and handle the technical side.

Do you only build software?

No. Some clients only need a website or an app. Others want more: ongoing support, help coordinating operations, or someone to manage the people and service providers involved. We take on as much or as little as you need.

What happens after launch?

That's up to you. You can run the solution yourself, or we can host, monitor, support and keep improving it for you, and grow into a broader partnership over time.
