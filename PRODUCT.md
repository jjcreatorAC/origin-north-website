# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses, mainly in Denmark, who run daily operations on a patchwork of spreadsheets, email threads and off-the-shelf tools that no longer fit how they work. They are not technical. They arrive from a referral, LinkedIn or search, usually on a laptop during the workday or on a phone in the evening, sceptical but hopeful.

Their job on this site: understand quickly what Origin North can build for them, judge whether the company is credible, and start a conversation about their own needs.

## Product Purpose

Marketing website for Origin North ApS (originnorth.dk), a Danish company that builds custom websites, apps and digital solutions around each client's needs: customer portals, internal dashboards, team management systems and tools that automate everyday work. Beyond software, Origin North can provide ongoing support, operational coordination and management of the people or service providers involved. Some clients need only a website or app; others want a broader partnership.

Success: qualified business owners contact info@originnorth.dk describing what they need.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quietly confident. Plain English, short sentences, concrete examples, no jargon. Nordic understatement. The name carries a navigational idea (start where the client is, the origin; head where they want to go, north) that is used with restraint, never as a gimmick.

Emotional goal: "these people understand how my business actually works, and they will be easy to work with."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates: gradient blob backgrounds, icon + title + text card grids, invented stats, logo walls.
- Too artsy to understand: experimental portfolio sites where a business owner cannot tell what is offered.
- Cold and technical: jargon, terminal or code aesthetics, developer-only feel.
- Corporate stock photography: handshakes, people smiling at laptops.

Positive references, chosen by the client:
- soliciabrown.com: mixed typography, fixed corner details, scroll-lit statements, pinned project showcase, horizontal movement.
- shift.vml.dev: section colours that shift as you scroll, animated halftone dot field, blur-in text, items orbiting a centred phrase.

## Design Principles

1. Clarity before craft. Every section states plainly what Origin North does before it tries to delight.
2. Show, don't claim. Illustrate what can be built (interfaces, flows, ways of working) instead of adjectives and invented numbers.
3. The client is the centre. The visitor's business sits at the middle of the story; copy and layout point back to their needs.
4. Calm precision. Few colours per section, generous space, exact alignment, motion that eases rather than performs.
5. Honest by default. No fabricated clients, testimonials, logos or statistics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard support, visible focus states, semantic landmarks and a skip link. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and all content is readable without JavaScript. Body text contrast of at least 4.5:1 on every section colour. Illustrative interface mockups are decorative and always paired with a plain-text description.
