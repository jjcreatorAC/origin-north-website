Privacy policy

How Origin North ApS handles personal data when you visit originnorth.dk or get in touch with us. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Origin North ApS, CVR 46680685, Denmark, is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@originnorth.dk.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and fonts are served from our own domain.

When you contact us, by email or through the project brief on this site, we receive the details you choose to send, typically:

  • your name, company, email address and phone number
  • what you write about your business, your needs and when you would like to start

The project brief is emailed to our inbox by this website itself; it doesn't pass through any outside form service.

This site is hosted by Hostinger, which may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site securely.

Why we use it

We use your details to answer your enquiry, prepare a proposal and, if we work together, to deliver our services. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to follow up on them, and for no longer than 12 months if no collaboration starts. Data relating to clients is kept for as long as the collaboration lasts and as required by Danish bookkeeping rules.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. It is only shared with providers that help us run our business, such as Hostinger, which hosts this website and our email.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, or ask for a copy in a portable format. Write to info@originnorth.dk.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) at datatilsynet.dk.
